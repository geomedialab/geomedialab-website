Dear Colleagues and friends,

You are warmly invited to the launch of the special issue of *Cartographica* (63.1), **Mapping Memories**, a collection of 13 contributions offering a range of perspectives on mapping memory. This special issue should be available very soon and is associated with an exhibition (see the [flyer](/attachments/cartographica-mapping-memories-flyer.pdf) for more details).

The event will take place on **October 15, from 10:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. EDT**, in person at Le Tiers Lieu, Institut national de la recherche scientifique (INRS), Montréal - Tiohtià:ke, or online via [Zoom](https://concordia-ca.zoom.us/my/sebastiencaquard). The event will feature brief presentations by the authors of each contribution, followed by a panel discussion and a Q&A.

Feel free to share this message along with the [flyer](/attachments/cartographica-mapping-memories-flyer.pdf) in your networks.

We hope to see you on October 15.

Sébastien, Léa and Sepideh
