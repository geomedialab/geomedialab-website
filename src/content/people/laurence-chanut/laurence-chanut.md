Laurence (she/they) was a graduate student exploring the intersections of human geography, music, and culture. Drawing from her experience as a DJ and dancer, she began investigating how the evolution of dance music can be mapped prior to the digital era. Her work aimed to bridge geography, media studies, sound design, and ethnomusicology, aiming to create an interactive visualization that connects the public with the histories of movement and sound.
