Daria Monjezi-Kochak is completing an Honours BA in Human Geography and First Peoples Studies at Concordia University. Her research, supervised by Dr. Sébastien Caquard and in collaboration with Dr. Tracie Barnett, explores how alternative mapping methods can be used to visualize relationships between the built environment, social networks, and health outcomes. Drawing on qualitative interview data and geospatial analysis, Daria’s work examines how urban design and park accessibility shape health and well-being in Montreal. Her broader interests include Indigenous land relations, environmental protection and conservation, and the role of outdoor education in fostering environmental awareness. Having spent her summers teaching at the Royal Botanical Gardens in Hamilton, Ontario, Daria is passionate about connecting people, especially young learners, to land-based knowledge and ecological stewardship.
