Léa Denieul-Pinsky works with Kanehsata'kehró:non Land Defenders to make maps and other data-driven visualizations for various projects including but not limited to land dispossession, illegal waste dumping and locating unmarked graves of children having attended religious institutions in Quebec. She is particularly interested in repatriating state-sanctioned colonial materials and repurposing them as “counter-archives” to support political change. After having completed her Ph.D at Concordia University in the Geography Department, she now works at the Institut National de la Recherche Scientique (INRS) as a post doc.
