Cameron is currently pursuing an MSc in Geography at Concordia University. His research revolves around literary geography, the digital humanities, web-mapping, and Québécois literature. He is interested in exploring the ways in which fictional/historical depictions of real cities affect how we perceive these urban spaces, and in developing new methods of analysing and visualising this relationship. During a BA in Urban Planning, he completed an honours thesis project mapping the emotional geography of a small selection of novels set in Montréal, using the narrative mapping platform Atlascine. After briefly getting some professional experience in urban planning, he has returned to begin a Masters, pivoting towards using natural language processing and other machine learning tools for further research in this field.
