Stefanie is a nature enthusiast, artist and mother, pursuing her masters at Concordia University. She returns to the lab after a 7-year hiatus, working as a guide and educator for kids in the nature connection as well as unschooling movements. Building off of her work in narrative and affective cartography during her honors undergrad and research work for Dr. Caquard, her research-creation thesis centers on mapping as a potential self-discovery practice for revealing the ways our selves and bodies relate to our surroundings. She is particularly curious about mapping relationships to non-human beings, landforms and weather. She will also be assisting in Dr. Caquard's SSHRC-funded project "(Re)tracer la mémoire cartographique".
