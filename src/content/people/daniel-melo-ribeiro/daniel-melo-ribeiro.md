PhD Student in Communication and Semiotics (PUC-SP). MSc in Digital Design (PUC-SP), PGDip in Information Management (UFMG) and BSc in Communications (UFMG). Member of the Peirce’s Studies International Center (CIEP/PUC-SP). Academic visitor at Geomedia Lab at Concordia University in Montréal/CA. Professional experience in knowledge management, interface design, information architecture and usability. Research interests: data visualization, information design, semiotics and cartography.
