Annie Thao Vy Nguyen (they/she) is a MSc student in Geography and Urban Studies at Concordia University, with a focus on queer geography. Using oral history, their thesis examines the changing geographies of queer Asian activism in Montreal, focusing on the shifting relationship between queer Asian activists, Chinatown, and the Gay Village, over time. Through this, Annie aims to document and investigate broader shifts in queer Asian activist histories across generations. Trained in Architecture and Philocreation dialogue facilitation, Annie curated and facilitated the oral history exhibition and program “Bodies Carrying: Traces & Stories” at the Centre for Oral History and Digital Storytelling, where they are now a core member.
