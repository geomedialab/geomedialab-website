Sepideh Shahamati is a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Communication at the University of Montreal, and a member of the Anti-Eviction Mapping Project in Parc-Extension and the Geomedia Lab at Concordia University. Her research explores the intersections of gentrification, storytelling, and cartography, with a particular focus on how personal narratives and memories can be used to map the broader impacts of gentrification and displacement. In her postdoctoral work, she examines the lived experiences of gentrification among residents of the Parc-Extension neighborhood in Montreal, with an emphasis on identifying displacement trajectories and the psychological impacts of gentrification. This research builds on her doctoral studies in the Department of Geography, Planning and Environment at Concordia University, where she focused on mapping the intangible heritage of Parc-Extension through narrative mapping.
